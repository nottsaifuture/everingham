THE EVERINGHAM

Make yourself at home.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan. Choose a floor to see inside. Select a room floor for its details, or click a door to open it.

Walkthrough begins on the front approach. Use WASD or arrows and drag to look. E opens a nearby door in the centre of your view. Touch controls provide a movement pad and door button. Return to entrance restores your starting point.

The supplied Langridge Homes plans govern the layout. Heights, stair dimensions and unseen details are inferred. Photographs guide the finishes; furniture and decorative choices are illustrative.
